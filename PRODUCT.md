# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local customers in Düsseldorf looking for professional nail care on mobile or desktop. They want to understand the salon’s services and prices quickly, see the quality and atmosphere, then book with minimal friction by WhatsApp or phone.

## Product Purpose

Present Thy & Vy Nails Düsseldorf as a trustworthy, refined local nail salon and turn visits into appointment enquiries. Success means visitors can confidently compare services, understand “ab” pricing, find the salon, and contact it in one or two taps.

## Brand Personality

Calm, meticulous, polished. The experience should feel welcoming and feminine without becoming sugary, ornate, or generic.

## Anti-references

Avoid pink-heavy beauty templates, generic equal-card landing pages, decorative glassmorphism, loud gradients, cluttered animation, and stock “luxury” clichés that make the salon feel interchangeable.

## Design Principles

- Make booking the natural next action at every high-intent moment.
- Build trust through clear prices, hygiene cues, real salon imagery, and complete contact details.
- Let calm pacing and craft details communicate quality.
- Keep essential information effortless to scan on a phone.
- Give the salon a distinctive Düsseldorf presence rather than a generic beauty-site identity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: strong text contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, touch targets of at least 44 px, meaningful image alternatives, reduced-motion support, and content that remains usable without JavaScript.
